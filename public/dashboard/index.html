Sell on Facebook.
Leave the listings to Meta’s Muse models.

Selling on social media means hours of admin for every item: cropping photos, writing descriptions, creating shop listings, then chasing buyers through comments and private messages. LiveLot automates that work. Show an item to your camera or upload a photo, check the listing, and publish.

  • No more cropping photos SAM 3.1 cuts the item out of the picture and Muse Image turns it into a clean product shot.
  • No more writing listings Muse Spark identifies the product, checks the details with a web search and writes the title and description. Muse Voice picks up the price you say.
  • No more chasing buyers LiveLot posts the item and adds it to your shop. The first person to comment sold gets a pay link by private message, and the item is held so it can’t be sold twice.
